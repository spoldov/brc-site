The ten weeks

Before the block starts we discuss your question and agree on a start date. Projects start four times a year. During the project the team is in touch with you every week.

  1. Week 1
    Intake at your organisation. The team and their supervisor visit to understand the problem.
  2. Week 3
    Action plan. Research question, approach and planning, for your approval.
  3. Weeks 4–7
    Desk research, data collection and analysis.
  4. Week 8
    Preliminary advice. First results, and your feedback before the team finalises.
  5. Weeks 9–10
    Report and presentation. The final advice report, presented to your decision-makers.
